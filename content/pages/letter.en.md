# Letter from the editor

C33 published its first story on 1 March 2026.

We began as a Chinese and French quarterly: one subject every three months, with room to follow it in depth. Issue 01, La fabrique, examined the distance between the stories fashion tells and the craft, decisions and commerce behind them. Issue 02, Le déplacement, followed fashion across cities, languages and audiences, asking what changes along the way.

In September, we expanded our fashion week coverage. During the spring/summer 2027 shows in Milan and Paris, we wrote about individual collections in Chinese and English. October brought a film section: the qipaos in In the Mood for Love, the act of looking in Portrait of a Lady on Fire, and the different routes by which films reach Cannes. On Instagram, @c33zine follows news in fashion, film and art.

Next, we will publish conversations with designers, costume designers and filmmakers working between China and Europe, alongside more reporting from shows, screenings and exhibitions. The first two issues remain online in their original Chinese and French. New work appears in Chinese and English, each version written for its own readers.

If you work in fashion or film and would like C33 to cover a show, screening or presentation, write to [contact@c33zine.com](mailto:contact@c33zine.com).

Kairos Zhang
Founder and editor-in-chief
Paris, 30 October 2026
