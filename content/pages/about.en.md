# About C33

C33 is an independent magazine covering fashion, art and culture. We also write about film, design and the people working across those fields. The website publishes in English and Chinese.

The name borrows +33, France's international dialling code. The C has more than one reading: Chine, code, caractère.

C33 published its first story on 1 March 2026. It began as a Chinese and French quarterly. Issue 01, La fabrique / 制造 (spring 2026), moved from the making of clothes to the stories fashion makes about them. Issue 02, Le déplacement / 位移 (summer 2026), followed fashion across cities, languages and markets. Both issues remain online in their original Chinese and French.

Since September 2026, the site has published work in English and Chinese on the Milan and Paris spring/summer 2027 shows, exhibitions, design and film.

## What we cover

- Fashion: collections, designers, craft and brands.
- Film: costume, festivals and the visual language of films.
- Art and design: exhibitions, objects and their place in everyday life.
- People: creators and public figures, their work and their public image.

## Editing and credits

C33 is edited by its founder, Kairos Zhang, who writes most of its stories and works with contributors on selected pieces. New work names its writer, publication date and image sources; factual corrections are noted at the end of the relevant article. Editorial and publishing details appear on the masthead.

If you find a factual error, write to contact@c33zine.com.

Instagram @c33zine shares images and updates. Subjects that call for more room become stories on the website.
